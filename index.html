About me

Geospatial science, artificial intelligence, and wildland fire.

My work sits where artificial intelligence, remote sensing, geospatial science, and wildland fire management meet. I build models and datasets that turn imagery, aircraft tracks, terrain, fuels, weather, and operational records into information that can support real decisions.

I earned my Ph.D. in Forest Sciences from Colorado State University in 2026, following an M.S. in Natural Resources from Cal Poly Humboldt and a B.S. in Fish, Wildlife, and Conservation Biology from CSU. Volunteering with Rist Canyon Volunteer Fire Department keeps my research tied to rural response and the realities behind the map.
